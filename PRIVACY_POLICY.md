# Privacy Policy for Travel Booking App

**Last updated: November 18, 2025**

## Introduction

Travel Booking App ("we", "our", or "us") operates the Travel Booking mobile application (the "Service").

This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you use our Service and the choices you have associated with that data.

## Information We Collect

### Personal Information
When you use our Service, we may collect the following personal information:

- **Full Name**: To identify you in booking records
- **Email Address**: For booking confirmations and account management
- **Phone Number**: To contact you regarding your bookings
- **Booking Details**: Travel routes, seat selections, departure dates, and payment information

### How We Collect Information
We collect information that you provide directly to us when you:
- Register for an account
- Make a booking
- Contact our customer support
- Use our services

## How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for the following purposes:

- **Service Delivery**: To process and manage your travel bookings
- **Communication**: To send booking confirmations, updates, and customer support
- **Account Management**: To maintain and manage your account
- **Service Improvement**: To improve our application and user experience
- **Security**: To protect against fraud and unauthorized access

## Data Storage and Security

- Your personal data is stored securely on our servers
- We implement appropriate technical and organizational security measures
- We use industry-standard encryption for sensitive data transmission
- We retain your data only as long as necessary to provide our services

## Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties.

We may share your information only in the following circumstances:
- **Travel Operators (PO)**: To fulfill your booking requests
- **Legal Requirements**: When required by law or to protect our rights
- **Service Providers**: With trusted partners who assist in operating our service (under strict confidentiality agreements)

## Your Rights

You have the right to:

- **Access**: Request a copy of your personal data
- **Correction**: Request correction of inaccurate data
- **Deletion**: Request deletion of your account and associated data
- **Withdrawal**: Withdraw consent for data processing at any time

To exercise these rights, please contact us at the email address below.

## Children's Privacy

Our Service is not intended for users under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal data, please contact us.
